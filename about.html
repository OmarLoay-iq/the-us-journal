A little more about us

A place for thoughtful reading

The Us Journal is an independent online publication for adult readers. Our focus is useful, non-explicit writing about relationships, marriage, and everyday life as a couple.

You can explore the collection by topic, search for a specific idea, or start with the featured read. There is no account to create and no email address to submit.

What you can expect

Clear language, thoughtful pacing, and content that respects the reader’s choices. Our articles suggest approaches to consider, not a formula that guarantees a particular outcome.

A transparent beginning

This launch collection was created with AI assistance. It is published as general editorial content, not work written or reviewed by a named therapist, physician, or other credentialed professional. No such review is claimed.

The publication currently has no paid membership, product checkout, active advertising, or affiliate links. Read our disclosures for the current status.
