A more comfortable read

Built for different ways of reading

The site uses semantic headings, descriptive links, keyboard-focus styles, a skip-to-content link, and text that can be enlarged. Decorative illustrations have empty alternative text; the main illustration has a description.

The reading collection is available without JavaScript. JavaScript adds local search, topic filtering, the mobile menu, a reading-progress indicator, and an optional copy-link button.

Choose your pace

Your device’s reduced-motion preference is respected. The site has no autoplay audio or video, no pop-up ads, and no automatic redirect. You can use your browser’s zoom and reading tools.

These design choices are not a claim of formal accessibility certification. If something is difficult to use, the content remains available as ordinary linked HTML pages.
